# MY COMPANY

## Goals

- **Audience & reach** — Grow visibility across LinkedIn, website, and events. Targets still being defined; direction is clear.
- **Pipeline growth** — Increase volume of qualified leads. This is the north star metric for 2026.
- **Conversion** — Improve lead-to-deal conversion rate across existing pipeline.
- **Brand positioning** — Become a recognized name in the space. Be the go-to, not one of many.

## Focus right now

- **Content engine** — Building a consistent content creation and publishing system, primarily LinkedIn and website/SEO.
- **Website & digital presence** — Optimizing the site and online footprint to drive inbound.
- **Agentic automation** — Automating internal processes with AI to run leaner and faster.

## Key channels

- LinkedIn (organic thought leadership and content)
- Website + SEO (inbound traffic)
- Events (in-person and industry presence)

## Saying no to

- Paid ads — betting on organic-only growth
- Social media breadth — no TikTok, X, Instagram, etc. LinkedIn is the focus.
- Low-fit clients — turning down work that doesn't match positioning
- Hyper-targeted email campaigns — stopped these. Broad offers with wider reach generate more pipeline than narrow, targeted lists that got no responses.

## Bets

- **AI/automation-first operations** — using AI where others hire headcount.
- **Organic-only growth** — no paid spend, proving it's not necessary to scale.
- **Robotics & Physical AI** — investing attention in this space when most in the field aren't.
