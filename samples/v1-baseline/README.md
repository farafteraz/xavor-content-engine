# V1 control sample

These unchanged August 2026 outputs preserve the v1 content engine as a control sample for comparison against v2. Compare the strategy, creative direction, calendar, and finished post text without regenerating this baseline.

Source: `output/2026-08/` at main commit `1e874d9994eec50cda8e495f7551d2bec6ca02d2`. All eight copied files are byte-for-byte identical to that revision; original filenames and post numbers are retained. Posts come from `posts/`, outside `drafts-v1/`.

## Selection

August is the latest coherent monthly sample found after reviewing July, August, and September. September contains multiple formats for some post numbers and mismatches between its calendar and final post angles. August's selected posts match its calendar and cover five formats and the governance, data, cost, and physical AI themes:

- 01: article stating the month's thesis.
- 03: explainer reel on AI cost attribution.
- 06: carousel on governed workflows.
- 07: video feature on physical AI integration.
- 11: static post on the data foundation.

Each selected post has a recorded QC PASS and an empty `verify_flags` list in its final QC memo (01 round 1, 03 round 1, 06 round 2, 07 round 1, 11 round 2). The [monthly QC report](https://github.com/farafteraz/xavor-content-engine/blob/1e874d9994eec50cda8e495f7551d2bec6ca02d2/output/2026-08/5-qc-report.md) records 13/15 passes and issues elsewhere in the month. Completion here means generated final text, including scripts and design notes; publication and media production are unverified. Existing claims and defects are preserved. This archive does not independently validate their factual accuracy.
