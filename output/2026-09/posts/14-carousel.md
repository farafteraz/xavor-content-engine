# Post 14 — 2026-09-23 — carousel
**Angle:** 35% of executives couldn't immediately shut down a rogue agent, and shadow AI now sits in 43% of breaches at a $4.99M average cost, because the agents were deployed without kill-switch wiring or an owner.

## Caption
An agent you can't shut down is an incident you can't stop. This year shadow AI showed up in 43% of breaches at a $4.99M average cost, and a third of executives admit they couldn't cut a rogue agent off if they had to. That gap is engineering work, and it's fixable before the board ever hears about it.

## Copy

**Slide 1:**
35% of executives say they couldn't immediately shut down a rogue agent. The agent is already deployed. The kill switch was never wired.

**Slide 2:**
Shadow AI showed up in 43% of breach incidents this year, up from roughly one in five the year before. Two-thirds of those organizations had no governance process at all.

**Slide 3:**
The global average breach cost hit $4.99M, a 12% jump and an all-time high. That is the number a CISO explains to the board when an unowned agent turns into an incident.

**Slide 4:**
The connection is direct. An agent deployed without an owner has no one watching it. An agent without a kill switch can't be stopped once it starts moving the wrong way.

**Slide 5:**
The fix is scope, not fear. Every production agent gets a named owner, a decision boundary, and a kill switch you've tested. That work happens before the incident, or during it.

**Slide 6:**
Wire the kill switch before the incident now. Get in touch.
