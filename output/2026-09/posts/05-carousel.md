## Caption
ServiceNow shipped AI Control Tower to GA in August as a five-dimensional solution. Discover, observe, govern, secure, measure. The console is done. The configuration across your actual agent estate is the part you own, and it decides whether any of the five dimensions produces an audit trail.

Five dimensions ship as capability. They become an operating layer only once they are wired to your estate. Turn the five into a configured estate now. Get in touch.
