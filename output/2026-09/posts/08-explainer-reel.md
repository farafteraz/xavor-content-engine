# Post 8 — 2026-09-11 — explainer reel
**Angle:** 60% of enterprises deploy AI across multiple departments but only 4% govern at scale, because the operating layer under the policy was never built.

## Caption
Governance at scale is infrastructure you wire into every agent, and most stacks were never built for it. Sixty percent deploy across departments; four percent govern at scale.

## Copy

Frame 1:
60% of enterprises now run AI across multiple departments. Only 4% govern any of it at scale. (Credo AI, 371 senior leaders)

Frame 2:
Enforcing a policy across every deployed agent takes an operating layer most stacks were never built to hold. That layer is what's missing.

Frame 3:
83% of IT leaders say their infrastructure needs upgrades for agentic AI. Only 17% are fully confident their stack can carry the agents they depend on. (Google, 1,400+ leaders)

Frame 4:
Policy lives in a document. Enforcement lives in wiring: inventory per agent, decision boundaries, token attribution, an audit trail that renders on demand.

Frame 5:
Governance urgency jumps from 61% to 92% the moment a program scales. That is when the unbuilt layer becomes the thing standing between you and production.

Frame 6:
Closing this is engineering work with a defined scope. Close the gap between deployed and governed now. Get in touch.
