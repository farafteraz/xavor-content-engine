**VIDEO FEATURE — 75 seconds**

**Beat 1 (0:00–0:10)**
We see: A Xavor engineer walking a factory floor, mid-conversation, gesturing toward a work cell where a humanoid places a component. Camera stays with the engineer, not the robot.
Line (VO): "The robot works. That part is settled. Figure ran 11 months at BMW Spartanburg, 30,000 vehicles, above 99% placement accuracy."

**Beat 2 (0:10–0:22)**
We see: Close on the engineer's hands at a laptop, a fleet dashboard filling the screen. Multiple units, live telemetry.
Line (VO): "The question a board should be asking isn't which humanoid. It's what carries the data off the floor, and where the decisions actually run."

**Beat 3 (0:22–0:36)**
We see: An edge compute node mounted near the line, cables traced back to the cell. Engineer points to it.
Line (VO): "Placement accuracy that high needs inference at the edge, not a round trip to a data center. We build the compute that sits beside the arm and answers in milliseconds."

**Beat 4 (0:36–0:50)**
We see: Two engineers at a shared screen, a data pipeline diagram, then a live feed of multiple units streaming into one view.
Line (VO): "One robot generates data. Forty generate a fleet. We build the pipelines that pull every unit into one signal your engineers can read and act on."

**Beat 5 (0:50–1:02)**
We see: A digital twin of the work cell on a wall display, the physical cell visible through a window behind it. The two move in step.
Line (VO): "The digital twin only earns its keep when it's wired to the real line. We connect the model to the machine, so what you test in simulation holds on the floor."

**Beat 6 (1:02–1:15)**
We see: Wide shot of the engineer looking out over the running cell, then to camera.
Line (VO): "The hardware capital is committed. The engineering that turns it into production is the work in front of you. Scope the layer around the robot now. Get in touch."
