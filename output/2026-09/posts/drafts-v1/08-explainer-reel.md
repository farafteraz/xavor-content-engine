# Post 8 — 2026-09-11 — explainer reel
**Angle:** 60% of enterprises deploy AI across multiple departments but only 4% govern at scale, because the operating layer under the policy was never built.

## Caption
Governance at scale is not a policy you write once. It is infrastructure you wire, and most stacks were never built for it. Sixty percent deploy across departments; four percent govern at scale.

## Copy

Frame 1:
60% of enterprises now run AI across multiple departments. Only 4% govern any of it at scale. (Credo AI, 371 senior leaders)

Frame 2:
The gap isn't a missing policy. Most companies wrote the policy. The operating layer that enforces it across the estate was never built.

Frame 3:
83% of IT leaders say their infrastructure needs upgrades to support agentic AI. Only 17% are confident their stack can carry a mission-critical agent. (Google, 1,400+ leaders)

Frame 4:
Policy lives in a document. Enforcement lives in wiring: inventory per agent, decision boundaries, token attribution, an audit trail that renders on demand.

Frame 5:
Governance urgency jumps from 61% to 92% the moment a program scales. That is the moment the unbuilt layer becomes the thing standing between you and production.

Frame 6:
This is engineering scope, not a procurement line. Close the gap between deployed and governed now. Get in touch.

## Evidence used
- [E52]: 60% deploying across multiple departments, only 4% governing at scale; governance urgency jumps 61%→92% at scaled programs (Credo AI, 371 senior leaders) — Frames 1 and 5.
- [E51]: 83% say infrastructure needs upgrades for agentic AI, only 17% fully confident their stack supports mission-critical agents (Google, 1,400+ senior IT leaders) — Frame 3.
