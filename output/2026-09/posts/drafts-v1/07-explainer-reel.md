# Post 7 — 2026-09-11 — explainer reel
**Angle:** An agent isn't in production until it has a named owner, a decision boundary, an escalation path, and a payback number attached to it.

## Caption
An agent running in production without an owner, a boundary, an escalation path, and a payback number isn't deployed. It's exposure sitting on your budget. Here are the four things to check before you call anything live.

## Copy

Frame 1:
You have agents running. The question your Q4 review will ask is simpler: who owns each one?

Frame 2:
Owner. Not the platform, not the vendor. A named person who answers when the agent does something wrong.

Frame 3:
Boundary. What the agent is allowed to decide, and where it has to stop and hand off. Write it down before launch.

Frame 4:
Escalation path. When the agent hits its limit, it needs a human on the other end and a route to reach them.

Frame 5:
Number. 88% of agent pilots never reach production. The ones that pay back do it in about five months. That's the metric to defend.

Frame 6:
Four things per agent, checked before you call it live. That's the difference between production and liability.

Frame 7:
Give every agent an owner and a number now. Get in touch.
