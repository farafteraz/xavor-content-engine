# Post 3 — 2026-09-03 — explainer reel
**Angle:** AI is now the largest unmanaged cost line in the enterprise: 98% of finance teams manage AI spend and 52% say no one owns it.

## Caption
Your finance team can see the AI spend. Ask who owns it and the room goes quiet. That gap between a number on the invoice and a name against it is where the money leaks.

## Copy

Frame 1:
Two years ago, 31% of finance teams tracked AI spend. Today it's 98%.

Frame 2:
Finance can see the AI number. It can't map that number to a team, a product, or a business unit.

Frame 3:
52% of finance teams say no one owns AI cost. Accountability splits four ways and lands nowhere.

Frame 4:
A line no one owns is a line no one meters, and a line no one meters only grows.

Frame 5:
When no one owns it, it gets funded by whatever it saves elsewhere, so no one ever right-sizes it. The number keeps climbing while the invoice keeps arriving.

Frame 6:
Ownership is engineering: token attribution mapped to team, product, and business unit. Now the spend has a name.

Frame 7:
Put an owner on the AI line now. Get in touch.

## Evidence used
- [E22]: Frame 1 — 98% of finance teams manage AI spend, up from 31% two years prior (2024 baseline, per the reconciled ledger).
- [E23]: Frame 3 — 52% say there's no clear owner of AI costs; accountability splits four ways.
- [E25]: Frame 2 — finance sees a spend number but can't map it to product, team, or business unit (token as the unmapped unit).
- [E27]: Frame 5 — organizations asked to self-fund AI investment through optimization savings; the "never right-sized, keeps growing" chain is our inference, written as argument.
