# Content Calendar — July 2026

## The big idea (one line, restated)
In 2026 the enterprise cannot buy its way out of the agent problem: every platform shipped the same autonomous capability at once, so the only differentiator left is whether you can sequence, govern, and account for what you deploy.

## Week 2: Where the gap bites — the project floor (2026-07-08 to 2026-07-14)

- **audience:** VPs of Engineering, data leads, AI delivery owners
- **cta:** Fix your scoping before your next pilot now. Get in touch.
- **evidence:** ["E14", "E15", "E16"]
- **why_this_slot:** Opens the week by naming the three root causes, so every following post this week has a diagnosis to build on.
