# Post 14 — 2026-07-23 — carousel
**Angle:** Organizations with fully integrated AI are nearly 4x more likely to report AI-driven revenue growth than those still piloting.

## Caption
Integrated AI runs inside how the business operates. That placement is what shows up in revenue. Grant Thornton found integrated shops report AI revenue growth at 58% against 15% for the ones still experimenting.

## Copy
Slide 1:
58% of companies with fully integrated AI report AI-driven revenue growth. Among the ones still piloting, it's 15%. That's nearly four times the odds (Grant Thornton, 2026).

Slide 2:
The revenue comes from placement: live data, a real workflow, an owned outcome. That's the variable the 58% got right.

Slide 3:
A pilot lives in a sandbox. It has no live data, no downstream system to act on, no one accountable for the outcome. It can be impressive and still change nothing.

Slide 4:
Integration is the unglamorous part: connect the model to real data, let it act inside a workflow, measure what it moves.

Slide 5:
McKinsey found nearly two-thirds of enterprises experimented with AI agents. Fewer than 10% scaled to tangible value. The revenue growth lives inside that last 10%.

Slide 6:
