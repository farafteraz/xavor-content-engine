# Post 6 — 2026-07-09 — explainer reel
**Angle:** Uber burned its entire 2026 AI coding budget in four months because a metered utility was budgeted as a fixed cost.

## Caption
Uber's 2026 AI coding budget lasted four months. The mistake wasn't overspending. It was budgeting a metered utility the way you budget a software license. Here's how to stop the same bill from surprising you.

## Copy

Frame 1:
Uber set its 2026 AI coding budget, then spent all of it in four months.

Frame 2:
Nothing broke. Engineers used the tools exactly as intended. The number just ran out.

Frame 3:
The budget assumed a flat annual license. The tool charged by the token, every prompt, every run.

Frame 4:
A per-seat line grows once a year at renewal. A metered line grows every time someone works.

Frame 5:
When cost tracks usage and usage tracks adoption, a successful rollout is what drains the account fastest.

Frame 6:
Yet 84% of enterprises haven't operationalized AI financial management, and 85% have no real-time view of the spend.

Frame 7:
So the fix is architectural: attribute every token to a team and an outcome on day one, before the bill lands.

Frame 8:
Meter your AI spend against outcomes now. Get in touch.
