**Angle:** Metered AI broke flat-rate budgeting, so cost attribution has to sit in the architecture on day one, not get reconstructed after the invoice.

## Caption
FinOps teams managing AI spend went from 31% to 98% in two years. The teams that will win the next budget cycle are the ones who wired attribution into the architecture before the meter started running.

## Copy

Slide 1:
Your AI bill is metered by the token. Your budget is still flat-rate. That mismatch is why the invoice arrives before anyone can explain what it bought.

Slide 2:
Uber burned its entire 2026 AI coding budget in four months. It had budgeted a metered utility as a fixed line item. The meter does not care about your annual plan.

Slide 3:
This is no longer a finance edge case. FinOps teams owning AI spend jumped from 31% in 2024 to 98% in 2026. The bill is already on every desk.

Slide 4:

Slide 5:
Attribution cannot be reconstructed from an invoice. Tag by team, workload, and outcome at the point of consumption, or you are guessing after the fact. Design it in, or reverse-engineer it forever.

Slide 6:
Build cost attribution into your architecture now. Get in touch.
