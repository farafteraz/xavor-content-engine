# Post 10 — 2026-07-16 — carousel
**Angle:** 78% of executives lack confidence they could pass an independent AI governance audit within 90 days, which is an operational readiness gap, not a paperwork gap.

## Caption
Give an independent auditor 90 days and full access to your AI systems. Would you pass? Most executives quietly answer no. They have the policies. What they lack is a way to reconstruct what every agent in production actually did, and who owns it.

## Copy

Slide 1:
78% of executives doubt they'd pass an independent AI audit in 90 days. The gap: proving what each agent did, and who owns it.

Slide 2:
An auditor asks who approved this agent, what data it touches, what it did last Tuesday at 2pm. Those are engineering questions.

Slide 3:
Most enterprises can't answer. Only 21% have mature governance for autonomous agents. The systems shipped faster than the ability to explain them.

Slide 4:
The 90-day question is whether your agents produce a trail an outsider could follow: decisions, data access, corrections, owners.

Slide 5:
That trail is engineering. Logging, access boundaries, evaluation records, a named owner per agent. Build it before the auditor asks.

Slide 6:
Close your 90-day audit gap now. Get in touch.

## Evidence used
- [E20]: opening stat, the 78% who lack confidence in passing an independent audit within 90 days (Grant Thornton 2026, n=950).
- [E51]: the 21% with mature governance for autonomous agents, used to show the readiness gap is operational (Deloitte 2026).
