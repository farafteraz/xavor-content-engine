**Angle:** The EU high-risk deadline moved out to December 2027 while operational governance risk got worse, so the urgency is real but it is not regulatory.

## Caption
Brussels gave you 18 more months on high-risk systems. The audit you could not pass today has not moved. If a Brussels deadline is what sets your governance work, most of the risk you carry is invisible to it.

Caption block (below the image, if the layout carries copy):
The EU AI Act Omnibus pushed the high-risk deadlines back. Annex III systems (recruitment, credit scoring, law enforcement) now have until December 2, 2027. That gave you calendar room on the compliance side, and nothing else changed. The agents you deployed this year still run every day. So ask a harder question than the one Brussels asks: could you pass an independent governance audit in the next 90 days? For 78% of executives, the honest answer is that they aren't confident they could. That gap is real today, and it is engineering and governance work, not a date on a European timeline.

Governance readiness will not wait for 2027. Get in touch.

## Evidence used
- [E54]: the Omnibus political agreement deferring Annex III high-risk deadlines (recruitment, credit scoring, law enforcement) to December 2, 2027, used as the compliance deadline that moved.
- [E20]: Grant Thornton finding that 78% of executives lack strong confidence they could pass an independent AI governance audit within 90 days, stated as lack of confidence (not inability), used as the operational risk that did not move with the deadline.
