# Content Calendar — October 2026

## The big idea (one line, restated)
Enterprise AI now fails in the space between governed platforms, not inside any one of them, and the single control posture that holds across Salesforce, ServiceNow, Snowflake, Databricks, and Oracle is the one thing no vendor sells.
