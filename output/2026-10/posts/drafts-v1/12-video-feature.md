# Post 12 — 2026-10-21 — video feature
**Angle:** Xavor's engineers configure the Harness, the Control Tower, and Unity Catalog to one posture in a single regulated estate, which is the integration work that turns five bounded control planes into one standard.

## Caption
Every platform governs its own agents well. None of them governs the next one over. Here is what it looks like when our engineers set one posture across all of them.

**Beat 1 (0:00–0:08)**
We see: An engineer at a desk, three monitors open. Left screen shows Salesforce admin, center ServiceNow, right a Databricks workspace. No stock-footage gloss. Real work.
VO: "A Fortune 500 estate runs agents in five places. Each vendor governs its own. The gap between them is where things break."

**Beat 2 (0:08–0:22)**
We see: Close on the center monitor. The engineer opens the Trusted Enterprise AI Harness configuration panel in Salesforce.
VO: "We start in the Harness. We set what agents can touch, what they can't, and who answers when one steps out of line."

**Beat 3 (0:22–0:36)**
We see: Cut to ServiceNow. The AI Control Tower view. The engineer maps the same rule set onto it, pointing at the policy fields.
VO: "Then the Control Tower. Same policy, same thresholds, same audit trail. Not a second standard bolted next to the first."

**Beat 4 (0:36–0:50)**
We see: Cut to Databricks. Unity Catalog permissions. A second engineer restricts a Genie Agent to its attached Sources, then shows the tool-call log.
VO: "In Unity Catalog we scope every agent to the data it's allowed to read. Every tool call it makes is logged against that scope."

**Beat 5 (0:50–1:02)**
We see: Wide shot, both engineers at a shared screen that lines up the three control planes side by side. They compare the policy on each.
VO: "Three platforms. One posture. When an auditor asks the same question in any of them, the answer matches."

**Beat 6 (1:02–1:15)**
We see: The lead engineer talking directly to camera, calm, in the workspace.
VO (on camera): "No vendor sells the thing that holds across all of them. That's integration work. Thirty years of it, most of it in regulated sectors where getting it wrong has a fine attached."

**Beat 7 (1:15–1:25)**
We see: The three-monitor setup again, now quiet and settled. Text resolves on screen: "One posture. Every platform."
VO: "See this delivered in your estate. Get in touch."
