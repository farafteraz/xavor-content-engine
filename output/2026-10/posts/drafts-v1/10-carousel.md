# Post 10 — 2026-10-16 — carousel
**Angle:** 98% of FinOps teams now manage AI spend and 72% of companies still exceeded cloud budgets last year, because a single agentic task crossing several platforms generates spend none of the per-vendor cost tools can attribute to a team or product.

## Caption
Your cost tools watch each platform. Your agents don't stay on one platform. That gap is where the budget goes, and no per-vendor dashboard can close it.

## Copy

**Slide 1:**
98% of FinOps teams now manage AI spend. 72% of companies still blew past their cloud budget last year. Both numbers are true for the same reason.

**Slide 2:**
Per-token prices keep falling. Total bills keep climbing. An agentic task doesn't spend tokens once. It spends them across every system it touches.

**Slide 3:**
One task crosses Salesforce, Snowflake, and Databricks. Each platform meters its own slice. None of them can tell you which team or product ran the task.

**Slide 4:**
A single agentic task can run past $1,000, with tokens shared across groups. Your per-vendor tools see three cost lines. They can't connect them to one owner.

**Slide 5:**
44% of companies report limited visibility while using cost tools. The tools work. They just stop at each platform's edge, and the task doesn't.

**Slide 6:**
Get token-level attribution across every platform, tied to the team and product that spent it. Get in touch.

- [E34]: Slide 1 and Slide 5 — 72% of global companies exceeded allocated cloud budgets last fiscal year; 44% report limited visibility despite using cost tools (FinOps Foundation).
- [E37]: Slide 4 — a single agentic task can cost $1,000+, tokens shared across groups (LinkedIn Pulse / Johan Sanneblad). Slide 2's price-down/bill-up framing draws on E31 within the N3 evidence base; the $1,000 figure is E37.
