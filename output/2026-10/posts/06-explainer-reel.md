# Post 6 — 2026-10-09 — explainer reel
**Angle:** 88.4% of enterprises took at least one agent-related security breach this year, led by data leakage and untrusted input manipulation, both of which surface where an agent crosses from one governed system into another.

## Caption
Almost nine in ten enterprises took an agent-related breach this year. The two most common failures both happen at the moment an agent leaves one governed system and enters the next, where no vendor's control plane reaches.

## Copy

Frame 1:
88.4% of enterprises took at least one AI agent security breach in the past twelve months. You probably filed yours as a platform problem.

Frame 2:
Look at where it happened. Data leakage hit 50.1% of them. Untrusted input manipulation hit 49.6%.

Frame 3:
Neither failure lives inside Salesforce, or Snowflake, or ServiceNow. Each of those governs its own estate well.

Frame 4:
Both failures live in the handoff. The moment an agent carries data from one governed system into the next, your policy stops and the next one hasn't started.

Frame 5:
That gap is where a leaked record slips out and where a poisoned input walks in. The breach you blamed on a platform happened between platforms.

Frame 6:
No vendor sells the control that spans the handoff. You have to configure it, across every system an agent touches.

Frame 7:
Close your agent handoff exposure now. Get in touch.
