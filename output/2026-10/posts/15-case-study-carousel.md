## Caption
Each enterprise platform governs the agents inside its own estate. The agent that crosses from Salesforce into ServiceNow into Databricks runs through three separate policies that were never reconciled. Here is the work we do in regulated stacks: setting one control posture across the Salesforce Harness, ServiceNow's Control Tower, and Databricks Unity Catalog, and where that posture has to be built by hand.
