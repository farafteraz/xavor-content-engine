# Post 5 — 2026-10-08 — carousel
**Angle:** 74% of leaders believe they could pass an AI compliance audit today while only 27% are fully mature, and the gap sits in the cross-platform handoffs no single vendor's control plane covers.

## Caption
Most audit confidence is scored against single systems. The decision a regulator actually examines runs across four of them. Where does your control posture stand when the handoff is the thing on trial?

## Copy

Slide 1:
74% of leaders say they could pass an AI compliance audit today. Only 27% are fully mature. The gap is the question worth sitting with.

Slide 2:
That confidence was scored one platform at a time. Each vendor governs its own estate well. None governs the handoff to its neighbor.

Slide 3:
A credit decision, a hiring screen, a triage call rarely lives in one system. It moves across several, each governed, each logging only its own slice.

Slide 4:
The regulator reads the whole decision, end to end. The weakest link is the space between your platforms, where no control plane reaches and no log is complete.

Slide 5:
That space is the 47-point gap. Thirty years of regulated-sector integration tells us where audits fall apart: the handoff, not the platform.

Slide 6:
Score your governance maturity against your cross-system handoffs, not one estate at a time. Score your governance maturity now. Get in touch.
