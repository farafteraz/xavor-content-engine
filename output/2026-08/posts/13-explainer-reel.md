# Post 13 — 2026-08-25 — explainer reel
**Angle:** The move from a funded governance line to a control map you can evidence at audit is a scoped sprint, and it's the demonstrable-control layer the platform leaves to you.

## Caption
Ninety percent of enterprises now fund AI governance. Twenty-seven percent are actually mature enough to prove it. The gap between funding a control and evidencing it at audit is a scoped piece of work, and we run it.

## Copy
Frame 1: You funded AI governance. 90% of enterprises did the same in 2026 (Schellman). Funding a line item and passing an audit are two different capabilities.

Frame 2: 74% believe they could pass a control-mapping audit today. Only 27% are actually mature enough to (Schellman). Belief is not evidence.

Frame 3: For autonomous agents the gap widens. Just one in five enterprises has a mature governance model for the agents already running in production (Deloitte).

Frame 4: The platform embeds high-risk AI into your workflows. It does not classify that AI, map your controls, or hand an auditor the trail. That part is yours.

Frame 5: Governance is now 8 to 12% of the average enterprise AI budget, up from 3 to 5% in 2024 (Deloitte). The spend moved. The demonstrable control often did not.

Frame 6: That distance is a scoped sprint, not a program. We map your high-risk systems, your controls, and the evidence each one produces, before an auditor asks.

Frame 7: Turn funded governance into demonstrable control. Book the readiness sprint now.

## Evidence used
- [E4]: 90% funded governance, 74% believe they could pass an audit, 27% fully mature (Schellman State of AI Governance 2026, n=525). Used in frames 1, 2, 5 framing.
- [E5]: ~21% (one in five) have a mature governance model for autonomous agents (Deloitte). Used in frame 3.
- [E6]: Governance now 8–12% of average enterprise AI budget, up from 3–5% in 2024 (Deloitte). Used in frame 5.

## Design note
Keep it text-forward on a plain dark ground, one stat per frame with the number set larger than the surrounding line so 90 / 74 / 27 read as a falling sequence across frames 1 and 2. Final frame drops the stats entirely and sits on the CTA in the brand's calmer type.
